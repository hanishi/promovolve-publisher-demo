A Large Study Ties Consistent Sleep Schedules to a Longer Life

Following more than two hundred thousand adults for a decade, researchers found that the regularity of a person's bedtime predicted mortality as powerfully as the total hours slept. The work pushes the science of rest beyond simple duration and toward the steadiness of the body's internal clock.

By Marguerite Adler
